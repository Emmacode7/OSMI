Own land, one square meter at a time.

Full plots cost more than most people can pay at once. The One Square Meter Initiative splits real, mapped plots into individual square meters, so you can start owning land today — 5 sqm at a time — and add more whenever you're ready.

How it works

From square meter to certificate, in minutes.

01

Create your account

Sign up once with your name, phone, and email — this is how we keep your allocations private and tied to you alone.

02

Choose a plot & sqm

Pick a plot and how many square meters to buy — minimum 5 sqm per purchase. See the live price and what's still available before you commit.

03

Pay securely

Checkout runs on Paystack — pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD. Your payment is verified directly with Paystack before anything is allocated to you.

04

Grow your ownership

Come back anytime to buy more. Prices only rise as infrastructure goes in — sqm you already own keeps the price you paid for it.
