The process

From square metre to certificate, in minutes.

01.

Create your account

Sign up once with your name, phone, and email — this is how we keep your allocations private and tied to you alone.

02.

Choose a plot & sqm

Pick a plot and how many square metres to buy — minimum 5 sqm per purchase. See live pricing and availability before you commit.

03.

Pay securely

Checkout runs on Paystack — pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD. Your payment is verified instantly before allocation.

04.

Grow your ownership

Come back anytime to buy more. Prices only rise as infrastructure goes in — sqm you already own keeps the price you paid for it.
