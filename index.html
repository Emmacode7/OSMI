How it works

From square meter to certificate, in minutes.

01

Choose a plot & sqm

Pick a plot and how many square meters to buy — minimum 5 sqm per purchase. See the live price and what's still available before you commit.

02

Pay securely

Checkout runs on Paystack — pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD. Your payment is verified directly with Paystack before anything is allocated to you.

03

Get your certificate

Your certificate of allocation lands in your email as a PDF, with your allocation ID and the exact price you paid — permanently on record.

04

Grow your ownership

Come back anytime to buy more. Prices only rise as infrastructure goes in — sqm you already own keeps the price you paid for it.
