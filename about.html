About

Leisure Court — Excellent Housing Solutions

Leisure Court Limited is a renowned multiple award-winning real estate company, founded with a vision to provide excellent housing solutions to all Nigerian residents and those in the diaspora. It was founded on the principle of professionalism, and passion and to serve as a bridge owing to the high deficit in housing in Nigeria. It is a household name in the Nigerian housing and real estate sector, and the winner of the excellent real estate brand of the year 2020 in the Nigerian housing awards and many other awards to its credit.

Leisure Court builds and develops residential land across the region. The One Square Meter Initiative is our answer to a simple problem: full plots are priced out of reach for a lot of people who could otherwise be building equity in land right now. Instead of requiring the full price upfront, we let you buy in by the square meter — starting at 5 sqm — and add to your holding whenever you're ready.

Every purchase is recorded permanently at the price you paid. As infrastructure — roads, drainage, and further development — goes into a plot, the price per square meter rises for new buyers, but never retroactively for sqm you already own. Buy once at ₦40,000/sqm, and that portion stays yours at that price even after the rate moves to ₦55,000 or higher.

You can check exactly what you own, and at what price, at any time — and if your plans change, you can request to sell back your square meters at the current rate.
